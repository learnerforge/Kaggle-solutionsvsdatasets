Purpose

An informational dashboard

This site displays records produced by an automation engine that works on public Kaggle competitions: scores, run attempts, requirement checks and rulebook versions. It exists to document an engineering experiment. It is not a product, a service or advice, and no account is offered.

Machine-generated content

Everything on the dashboard is generated automatically. Scores and metrics are measured artifacts of that automation. They may be incomplete, stale or wrong, and they can change whenever the engine runs again. Verify anything that matters against the primary sources: the competition pages on Kaggle and the files in the repository.
